## Summary

Google announced Gemini 4 Argon, its new frontier model for coding, knowledge work, and cybersecurity, with a one-million-token output limit and an initial rollout to trusted cyber defenders through the Fairwind Program. Google DeepMind introduced protein watermarking based on SynthID to help identify AI-designed protein sequences without compromising their function, while Google also launched the open, multimodal EmbeddingGemma 2 for on-device search and retrieval. Google began replacing Gems with globally available Skills in Gemini, and announced tighter model access tiers for personal users beginning in October. The company also advanced Project Suncatcher by sending a TPU-equipped satellite into orbit to test AI hardware in space.
